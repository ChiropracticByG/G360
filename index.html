A focused training, cardio and nutrition tracker. No account, no ads, no noise — everything lives right here on your device.

Data & Privacy
All training, cardio and nutrition data is stored locally in this browser using localStorage — nothing is sent to a server. The app works fully offline. Food search can optionally reach Open Food Facts (a free, public nutrition database) when you're online; if there's no connection, the built-in food list is used instead. Export a backup any time to move your data to another device.
